# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Lexync serves independent language learners who deliberately collect useful words and phrases while reading, then return to practise them. The primary workflow crosses the Chromium extension and authenticated web client; public and authentication routes introduce the same product to prospective and returning learners.

## Product Purpose

Lexync turns language encountered in real reading into private, synchronized learning material. Success means a learner can capture with little interruption, understand the active Learning Language at every step, and return to a focused review or library task without losing context.

## Positioning

Keep the language you choose, in the context where it became meaningful.

## Brand Personality

Focused, curious, and reassuring. The fox identity should feel observant and intelligent without becoming childish, ornamental, or loud.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic AI-generated editorial landing page, a default unstyled HTML application, or a compressed dashboard made from repeated bordered cards. Avoid oversized display typography in task flows, numbered section labels, repeated uppercase kickers, ruled-column scaffolding, browser-default dropdowns, wrapping desktop headers, decorative gradients, and arbitrary motion.

## Design Principles

- Make the reading-to-learning loop visible instead of describing it abstractly.
- Keep language context persistent and immediately understandable across surfaces.
- Give every screen one dominant task and let supporting actions recede.
- Use the fox artwork as identity and orientation, not as decoration repeated everywhere.
- Earn visual character through composition, typography, and interaction quality rather than ornamental containers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, names, states, and error communication. Preserve usability at 200 percent text scaling, prevent horizontal overflow, honor reduced-motion preferences, and keep touch targets reachable on phone-sized surfaces.
