# Lexync Domain Glossary

## Learner

A person who owns private learning material, language settings, and learning progress.

## Target Language

A language variety the Learner is studying, identified by a BCP 47 language tag.

## Reference Language

A language variety the Learner uses to understand a Target Language, identified by a BCP 47 language tag.

## Study Pair

The unique combination of one Target Language and one Reference Language belonging to a Learner. A Learner chooses one Study Pair as primary for each Target Language. Its Target Language and Reference Language cannot change after it owns a Vocabulary Entry.

## Expression

A word or phrase in a Target Language. Capitalization, Unicode representation, and insignificant whitespace do not create separate Expressions. Linguistically distinct surface forms remain separate Expressions; Lexync does not normalize them to a lemma.

## Vocabulary Entry

A Learner's private record of an Expression within one Study Pair. It owns the Learner's Senses, Examples, Collection memberships, suspension state, and learning progress.

## Sense

A Learner-defined meaning of a Vocabulary Entry. Each Sense has one or more translations in the Study Pair's Reference Language and its own Examples and Cards.

## Example

A sentence showing an Expression in context. An Example is private unless it is explicitly published in a later sharing workflow.

## Card

A reviewable direction of one Sense. Recognition presents the Target Language and asks for the Reference Language; recall presents the Reference Language and asks for the Target Language.

## Collection

A flat, Learner-owned grouping of Vocabulary Entries within one Study Pair. A Vocabulary Entry may belong to multiple Collections. Collections do not contain other Collections.

## Learning Mode

An extension mode enabled by the Learner for a website. It exposes saved Expressions and offers capture actions for unsaved words and selected phrases.

## Scheduled Review

Practice of Cards currently due under the Learner's spaced-repetition schedule. Its results update future scheduling.

## Free Practice

Learner-initiated practice for a Collection. Its results do not change spaced-repetition schedules.

## Suspended Vocabulary Entry

A Vocabulary Entry retained as known learning material but excluded from Scheduled Reviews until resumed.
